03 / Litbuy shopping guide

Less guesswork.
Better comparisons.

A Litbuy spreadsheet guide is most useful when it helps you make a decision. Start with a specific need, keep the original listing attached and compare equivalent variants.

A focused shortlist is easier to maintain than a long collection of unexplained links.

Start your research ↗

  1. Choose one category and a clear brief

    Specify the item, preferred fit, color and intended use. “Relaxed cotton T-shirt” gives you a clearer comparison than “popular clothing.”

  2. Save the original product reference

    Keep the full listing URL, seller name and selected variant together. An image alone can be difficult to trace later.

  3. Check measurements and total cost

    Compare size charts with an item you own. Separate item price from any delivery, service or destination charges that apply.

  4. Recheck the listing before ordering

    Confirm stock, current price, materials and terms on the original source. Mark the check date so older notes are easy to identify.

04 / Litbuy product links

What belongs in a useful row?

Use this reference when building your own product comparison sheet or reviewing a community list.

Field What to record Why it helps
Product and category A descriptive name, item type and intended use. Keeps unrelated items out of the same comparison.
Original URL and seller The source listing and the seller shown there. Lets you return to the evidence behind the entry.
Variant and measurements Color, size, dimensions and measurement units. Reduces confusion between different versions.
Price and currency The displayed price for the selected variant. Makes price comparisons more consistent.
Additional costs Known delivery or service charges; unknown costs marked clearly. Prevents an item-only price from being mistaken for a final total.
Evidence and check date Listing notes, available detail photos and the date reviewed. Separates checked information from assumptions and stale entries.

For a Litbuy clothing spreadsheet

Compare chest width, length, shoulder width and fabric details where available. For pants, add waist, rise and inseam. Treat a size label as a starting point and check how the seller measured the garment.

For a Litbuy shoes spreadsheet

Keep the stated sizing system, available length guidance and intended use together. Different shoes may fit differently, so a familiar size number alone is not enough to establish fit.

05 / Direct answers

Before you
follow a link.

Practical answers for readers comparing a Litbuy finds spreadsheet, reviewing saved products or preparing their first shortlist.

Browse CAFANS ↗
What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

In shopping searches, a Litbuy spreadsheet generally means an organized list of product references intended to help shoppers compare items. Useful fields include the original product URL, category, seller, selected variant, price, size notes and the date checked.

Does this page provide an official Litbuy spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent guide with category links to CAFANS. It does not provide an official Litbuy document, account service or verified live inventory.

How do I check whether a product link is useful?

Open the original listing and compare its images, seller details, available variants, measurements, current price and return terms. Record when you checked it, because a saved spreadsheet row may become outdated.

Can a spreadsheet confirm product authenticity?

No. A spreadsheet entry, brand name or photograph does not establish authenticity. Check the seller’s authorization and supporting information, and use official or authorized retailers when authenticity is essential.

What should I include in a clothing comparison?

Include garment measurements, fabric information, fit, selected color, size, item price and any known delivery costs. Compare measurements with clothing you already own instead of relying only on size labels.

Where do the category links go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. General browsing links open the CAFANS homepage.
